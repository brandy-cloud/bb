Tribal Nation Construction Consulting

About B&B Business Solutions

Founded in 2025 by Billy and Brandy Shapp. Northeast Oklahoma roots. Serving OK, MO, AR, and KS.

Three Nations. One Mission.

Billy and Brandy are both Native Americans, proudly enrolled members spanning three tribal nations: the Quapaw Nation, the Miami Tribe of Oklahoma, and the Seneca-Cayuga Nation. That heritage shapes how we work: with integrity, with respect for sovereignty, and with a deep commitment to the communities we serve.

Billy Shapp

Business Development, Operations & Construction

Billy grew up in Quapaw, Oklahoma, and played baseball at Seminole State College before building a career in business development, sales, customer service, and economic development. He leads B&B's small business consulting practice, working directly with entrepreneurs and established businesses on strategy, operations, and growth. On the construction side, Billy has hands-on experience as an owner's representative and site superintendent. He also serves on a board that oversees business operations and expansion for a Native American Nation.

Brandy Shapp

Construction Advisory & Marketing

Originally from Kansas, Brandy attended Missouri Southern State University and Texas Woman's University before building her career in construction and marketing. She leads B&B's construction division with experience in project compliance, owner's representation, contract administration, and procurement. She works with tribal nation construction projects, casino and hotel developments, and AIA contract compliance. Brandy also brings 12+ years in the marketing industry spanning B2B and B2C sales, website design and development, and AI implementation for small businesses.

Our Philosophy

After raising seven kids (all adults now) and welcoming four grandchildren, we know what it means to build something that lasts. We created B&B to be the resource we wished existed when we needed it: expert guidance without big-firm overhead, delivered by people who actually do the work. Every client gets the principals, not junior staff.

We don't just fix problems and leave. We build your capacity to manage them going forward. Our clients walk away with systems, knowledge, and confidence, not a dependency on consultants. And we are always transparent: our proposals are open books, and if the scope shifts, we work with you to adjust. No surprises, no hidden fees.
